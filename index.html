Museo del Galeón

Welcome to Museo del Galeón, where stories, artifacts, and experiences bring the past closer to the present. As you step inside, explore history through a different perspective, where even visitors who are blind or visually impaired can imagine each exhibit through thoughtful descriptions and discover the stories beyond what the eyes can see.

Where the History A waits

Located at Seaside Boulevard corner Sunset Avenue, SM Mall of Asia Complex, Pasay City, Museo del Galeón takes visitors back to the era of the Manila-Acapulco Galleon Trade. Its exhibits highlight how maritime trade connected the Philippines to distant lands through the exchange of goods, ideas, and culture, shaping the country’s history and cultural heritage.

Beyond its displays, the museum offers a glimpse into the lives of our ancestors, whose strength, courage, and resourcefulness helped them navigate the challenges of their time. Their livelihoods, craftsmanship, and interactions with other societies helped shape the foundations of future generations. Through interactive experiences, such as discovering the scents of spices and learning traditional rope-tying techniques, visitors can engage with history in a more meaningful way. Each exhibit serves as a reminder of how the past continues to influence the culture, traditions, and heritage we carry today.
